# TWISTERS
 TWISTERS
Minecraft weather reimagined.

Have you ever been playing Minecraft and felt like the weather was too uncommon, too predictable, and too boring?

TWISTERS is a severe-weather-focused modpack built around ProtoManly's Weather that turns Minecraft's atmosphere into an active part of the game.

Watch storms develop, track dangerous weather, intercept tornadoes, receive emergency alerts, and deal with the aftermath when severe weather hits your world.

Whether you want to storm chase, survive destructive tornadoes, watch supercells develop, or just have a world where the weather actually feels alive, TWISTERS is built around that experience.

 Weather
 Tornadoes — Severe storms can produce tornadoes capable of damaging the world around them.
 
 Supercells — Watch organized thunderstorms develop and evolve.
 
 Squall Lines — Storms aren't limited to isolated cells; larger organized systems can develop.

 Emergency Alerts — EAS integration provides warnings for dangerous weather.

 Dynamic Atmosphere — Weather develops and changes rather than simply switching between clear skies and rain.

 Wildfire Additions — Additional environmental hazards expand the effects of extreme conditions. This may include fires destroying villages, fire tornadoes, and forests burning down.

 Natural Regrowth — Damaged environments don't necessarily stay destroyed forever.

 Villagers React to Weather — Villagers can seek cover when dangerous storms approach.

Elton's Wind Meter — Adds more anemometers, wind cup vanes, tornado probes, and enhanced detail of wind and atmosphere.

Exposure - A mod that lets you photograph storms and store them in your base, with the ability to rate them based on damage.

TWISTERS isn't only about hiding.

Chase the storm.

Tornado Intercept expands the storm-chasing side of the pack, while vehicle and aircraft additions give you more ways to explore your world and experience severe weather.

Track storms, get into position, and watch or intercept the tornadoes.

 Immersion

Weather should feel dangerous, not just look different.

TWISTERS includes enhancements to sound physics, dynamic lighting, aerodynamics and other environmental effects to make storms and the surrounding world more immersive.

Thunder in the distance might be worth paying attention to.

 Exploration

The pack still leaves room for normal Minecraft survival and exploration.

Expanded structures through Towns and Towers, vehicles, aircraft and quality-of-life additions give you things to do between storms without taking the focus away from weather.


 Performance

Severe weather can be demanding, so TWISTERS includes a collection of optimization mods intended to improve performance, including optimizations for rendering, entities, memory usage, chunk management and general game performance.

⚠️ Emergency Alert Audio Warning

TWISTERS includes realistic in-game weather alerts with loud emergency attention tones, warning announcements, and the names of affected in-game towns.

These are simulated in-game alerts and are NOT real-world emergency warnings.

The attention tones may be very loud, especially with headphones. Please adjust your volume as needed and avoid playing the alert audio loudly in public or anywhere it could be mistaken for a real emergency broadcast.
